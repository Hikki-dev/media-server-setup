Why this exists

A media server is thirteen apps that have to talk to each other.

Each one does a small job well. Wiring them together is the hard part. This does the wiring for you.

By hand

  • ×Install each app one at a time, and learn what it does.
  • ×Find every app’s key and paste it into the others.
  • ×Set up search sites, the downloader, folders and subtitles yourself.
  • ×Hope it works, and find out only when nothing arrives.

With Media Server Setup

  • Answer a few questions in your browser, in plain words.

How it works

Ask for a title. Watch it. In between, nobody lifts a finger.

One request travels through five apps. They are already connected, so it just arrives.

  1. 01 · AskSeerrFamily and friends ask for something to watch.
  2. 02 · FindProwlarr, Sonarr, RadarrSearch sites are checked and the right release is picked.
  3. 03 · DownloadqBittorrentDownloads it, then stops uploading once it is in your library.
  4. 04 · TidyBazarrFinds subtitles, English first.
  5. 05 · WatchJellyfinMovies, shows, anime and live TV on any device.

Then, when the download is finished, it is deleted from the downloader so nothing keeps uploading. Comics and manga take the same road, through Kapowarr and Kaizoku.

How easy it is

Step 1 · under a minute

Run one line

It checks Docker, downloads the setup, and prints a web address. On Windows, double click one file instead.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hikki-dev/media-server-setup/main/install.sh | bash
Checking Docker…

Step 2 · about 5 minutes

Answer a few questions

In your browser, in plain words. What you watch, where files live, which apps to add. Every choice has a sensible default.

Step 3 · 5 to 15 minutes

It does the rest

Check your choices, press install, and go and make tea. It installs every app, links them, then runs a real search to prove it works.

Questions

The things people ask first.

What do I need?

A computer with Docker. The install command checks for it and shows its download page if it is missing.

Is it free?

Yes. It is free and open source, under the MIT licence.

Does it host or provide media?

No. It installs and connects other free programs on your own computer. Use it only for content you have the right to.

What if I stop halfway?

Stopping is always safe, and your media is never touched.

Can I change my choices later?

Yes. You can change any of the settings later without installing again.

Do I need to understand the apps?

No. Everything is explained in plain words, with sensible defaults already chosen.
